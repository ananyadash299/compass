The ranking formula

score = BM25(query, doc) × recency_boost(doc)

recency_boost = 0.7 + 0.6 × (0.5 ^ age_days / 180)

→ A doc updated today gets a ~1.3× boost. A 3-year-old doc gets ~0.7×. Freshness never trumps relevance — it just breaks ties the right way.

Why this beats "just ask ChatGPT"

🔒 Grounded, not guessed

The LLM only sees your bank's docs. It can't invent a policy that doesn't exist.

🕒 Always current

Public LLMs are frozen at training time. Compass reads live from your source of truth.

📎 Fully traceable

Every sentence links to a source ID. An auditor can reconstruct exactly why we said it.

Model choice: Today, a rule-based synthesizer (no external model — clean, deterministic demo). Prod path: any enterprise-hosted LLM — Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock (Claude / Titan), or on-prem Llama-3 / Mistral. The RAG pipeline is model-agnostic — swap the brain, keep the guardrails.
This is the technical depth slide. Walk it in order. (1) Understand — we parse intent, not just keywords. (2) Retrieve — BM25 today, vector embeddings next. (3) Re-rank — our secret sauce: exponential freshness decay, half-life 180 days. (4) Ground — the LLM only sees the passages we selected, so it can't hallucinate outside our corpus. (5) Cite — anything not mappable to a source ID is dropped. And the model itself is swappable — Azure OpenAI, Bedrock, on-prem Llama — the pipeline doesn't care. If someone asks about hallucination, point at steps 4 and 5.
